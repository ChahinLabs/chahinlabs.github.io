Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chahin Labs (operated by Abdel Chahin, Taiwan; "we", "us") handles information in connection with this website and our two uploader tools:

  • SabrosuraUploader for YouTube (the "YouTube Uploader"), and
  • Sabrosura Uploader for TikTok (the "TikTok Uploader"),

together, the "Tools". The Tools are private desktop utilities that run only on our own computer. They are used only by Chahin Labs to upload our own original gameplay videos to our own accounts: the YouTube channel SabrosuraSmash (@sabrosurasmash) and the TikTok account @captainfalcon99. They are not offered to the public and have no other users.

1. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or any third-party scripts, and it has no forms or sign-ups. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs under its own policy: GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. Information the Tools access

YouTube Uploader (YouTube API Services)

With the account owner's permission through Google OAuth, the YouTube Uploader uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the authorized channel, with the title, description, and privacy setting we choose; and
  • read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel is signed in.

The YouTube Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

TikTok Uploader (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API)

With the account owner's permission through TikTok Login Kit, the TikTok Uploader requests only these scopes:

  • user.info.basic: to read the basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and confirm the correct TikTok account is signed in; and
  • video.upload: to send a finished video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft. The account owner then edits, captions, chooses who can see it, and posts it inside the TikTok app.

The TikTok Uploader does not post publicly on its own, does not read followers, messages, comments, or analytics, and does not access any other user's data. TikTok's own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of TikTok: TikTok Terms of Service, TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. What we store and where

  • Access and refresh tokens from Google and TikTok are stored only in local files on the owner's own PC. They are never uploaded to cloud storage, shared, or sent anywhere other than back to Google or TikTok to make the API requests described above.
  • Upload records: the video ID (YouTube) or publish ID (TikTok) returned after each upload, the file name, and the time are kept in a local log so the same video is not uploaded twice.
  • App credentials (client IDs and secrets) are stored only on the owner's PC and are kept confidential.

We do not store passwords. Sign-in happens on Google's and TikTok's own pages.

4. How we use and share information

Information is used only to upload our own videos to our own accounts and to confirm the right account is signed in. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics. The only data transfers are the API requests to Google (YouTube) and TikTok needed to perform the upload.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the Tools are in use and are deleted from the PC when access is revoked or the Tools are retired. Upload logs are kept as long as the related videos exist on our channels. To request deletion of any stored data, email chahinlabs@gmail.com. We respond within 7 days.

6. How to revoke access

  • Google / YouTube: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services), select the uploader app, and remove its access.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Profile → Menu (☰) → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions (wording may vary by app version), select Sabrosura Uploader, and remove access.

Once access is revoked, any stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the local PC.

7. Children

The Tools and this website are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Security

Tokens and credentials are stored on a single password-protected personal computer and are transmitted only over HTTPS to Google and TikTok.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
