Smash clips with headlines they don't deserve.

Chahin Labs is a one-person creator studio in Taiwan run by Abdel Chahin. Our channel, SabrosuraSmash, turns original Super Smash Bros. Ultimate gameplay into short videos: real matches, real finishing hits, and breaking-news-style captions like "Drunk driver runs over hedgehog."

Watch the Shorts on YouTube YouTube channel

What we make

  • Short vertical videos (under 60 seconds) cut from our own recorded matches.
  • Captain Falcon, Joker, and whoever is unlucky enough to be on the other side.
  • Original game audio, no reposted content from other creators.

Our tools

To spend more time playing and less time clicking upload buttons, Chahin Labs uses two small in-house tools that run only on the creator's own editing PC:

  • YouTube uploader: uploads finished Shorts to the SabrosuraSmash YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Sabrosura Uploader for TikTok: sends finished videos to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts (inbox uploads). The creator then reviews, captions, and posts each one inside the TikTok app.

Both tools are used only by Chahin Labs for its own accounts. They do not collect data from viewers or other users. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
